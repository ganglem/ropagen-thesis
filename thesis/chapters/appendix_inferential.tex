\chapter{Inferential Statistics in Full}
\label{app:inferential}

This appendix reproduces the complete inferential test output referenced in
Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation}. Sample-size definitions follow
Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}: the within-subjects survey tests use $n = 69$,
the within-subjects document-quality tests use the matched-triple subset of
$n = 26$, and between-groups sensitivity checks on the unbalanced full
sample use $n = 113$.

\subsection{Mode Preference Ranking}

A chi-square goodness-of-fit test on the rank-1 vote distribution against a
uniform null gives $\chi^2(2, N = 69) = 14.17$, $p < 0.001$, Cramér's
$V = 0.320$ (medium effect). The Friedman test on the full rank scores
($1$--$3$) is non-significant: $\chi^2(2) = 5.07$, $p = 0.079$, $W = 0.037$.
