\section{Cognitive Load — NASA Raw Task Load Index}
\label{sec:eval-rtlx}

The Raw Task Load Index captures six dimensions of perceived workload — Mental
Demand, Physical Demand, Temporal Demand, Performance, Effort, and Frustration
— each on a five-point Likert scale. Performance is reported in the
positive-coded direction (higher equals worse perceived performance) for
internal consistency with the other subscales. The composite R-TLX in this
study is the unweighted mean of the six subscales, rescaled to a $1$--$21$
range.

deviation per mode and the aggregated mean across modes.
Figure~\ref{fig:rtlx-subquestions} shows the corresponding distributions.

For \emph{Mental Demand}, Form scores lowest (mean $5.77$), Chat next ($6.36$),
and Ask highest ($6.80$); the aggregated mean is $6.31$. Mental Demand
increases by approximately $0.6$--$1.0$~points when moving from Form to the
LLM-driven modes.

For \emph{Physical Demand}, Form scores lowest ($2.77$), Chat second ($3.26$),
Ask highest ($3.54$); aggregated $3.19$. The absolute spread is small
($\approx 0.8$~points). Physical Demand is the lowest-rated subscale in all
three modes.

For \emph{Temporal Demand}, the values increase monotonically from Form ($4.16$)
to Ask ($4.97$) to Chat ($5.54$); aggregated $4.89$. Temporal Demand is
approximately $1.4$~points higher in Chat than in Form, the largest monotonic
increase observed across the six subscales.

For \emph{Effort}, Form scores lowest ($5.68$), with Ask ($6.39$) and Chat
($6.32$) at a closely comparable level; aggregated $6.13$. Effort increases
by approximately $0.65$~points from Form to the LLM-driven modes.

For \emph{Frustration}, Form scores lowest ($5.88$), Chat second ($7.13$), and
Ask highest ($7.58$); aggregated $6.86$. Frustration shows the largest
absolute gap between Form and the LLM-driven modes ($+1.25$ to $+1.70$~points).

For \emph{Performance} (positive-coded, higher equals worse perceived
performance), Form and Ask are essentially identical ($8.71$ and $8.68$); Chat
is slightly higher at $9.39$; aggregated $8.93$. Perceived self-performance
is comparable across modes, with a marginal degradation in Chat.

\subsection{Composite R-TLX Score}

The composite R-TLX is reported in Table~\ref{tab:rtlx-composite} and
Figure~\ref{fig:rtlx-aggregate}. Form yields the lowest composite ($5.50$),
Ask and Chat yield identical higher values ($6.33$ each); the aggregated mean
is $6.05$. The composite increases by approximately $0.83$~points from Form
to the LLM-driven modes.

The Friedman omnibus on the composite R-TLX is non-significant
($\chi^2(2) = 5.24$, $p = 0.073$, $W = 0.038$). Among the six subscales, two
reach significance: Temporal Demand ($\chi^2(2) = 12.77$, $p = 0.002$,
$W = 0.093$) and Frustration ($\chi^2(2) = 8.93$, $p = 0.012$, $W = 0.065$).
Pairwise post-hoc Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction
identify Form versus Ask and Form versus Chat as significant for both the
composite and these two subscales, with medium-to-large rank-biserial effect
sizes ($|r| = 0.36$ to $0.57$); Ask versus Chat is non-significant on every
measure. Full test statistics are reproduced in
Appendix~\ref{app:inferential}.
