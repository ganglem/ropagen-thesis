# Interpretive Summary of LLM-as-Judge Scores Across ROPA Generation Modes

## 1. Overall Mode Differences

Across the five evaluated dimensions, the three interaction modes — Form, Ask, and Chat — exhibit meaningfully distinct scoring profiles, with no single mode dominating uniformly. Form achieves the highest mean completeness (4.838) and legal correctness (3.595), suggesting that structured questionnaire-based interaction reliably elicits the requisite GDPR fields and legally appropriate framing. However, Form simultaneously records the lowest mean scores on scenario faithfulness (1.838) and hallucination_inverse (1.649), indicating that structural completeness is frequently purchased at the cost of factual fidelity to the provided scenario. Ask occupies a middle position on most dimensions, with a completeness mean of 3.838 and legal correctness of 3.432, while Chat scores lowest on completeness (3.179) and legal correctness (3.154) but achieves the highest hallucination_inverse mean (2.538) and scenario faithfulness (2.487) of the three modes. Overall scores are comparably low across all modes — Form: 2.405, Ask: 2.486, Chat: 2.615 — indicating that no mode consistently produces documents of high holistic quality, and that the dimensions contributing to overall scores are frequently in tension with one another.

## 2. Completeness vs. Faithfulness Trade-off

The data reveal a pronounced inverse relationship between structural completeness and scenario faithfulness. Form's mean completeness of 4.838 — nearly a full point above Ask (3.838) and 1.66 points above Chat (3.179) — is accompanied by the lowest scenario faithfulness mean (1.838), compared to Chat's 2.487 and Ask's 2.297. This pattern suggests that Form-mode participants, guided by a comprehensive questionnaire, produced documents that populate all required ROPA fields but frequently filled those fields with fabricated or contextually inappropriate content rather than information grounded in the provided scenario. The median completeness scores reinforce this: both Form and Ask achieve a median of 5.0, while Chat reaches only 3.0, yet Form's median scenario faithfulness (2.0) is no better than the other modes. The structural scaffolding of the Form interface thus appears to encourage comprehensive document architecture while simultaneously decoupling content from the specific processing context participants were asked to document.

## 3. Hallucination Profile

Form exhibits the most severe hallucination tendency, with a mean hallucination_inverse score of 1.649 — the lowest of the three modes and notably distant from Chat (2.538) and Ask (2.405). Since hallucination_inverse is scored such that higher values indicate less hallucination, Form-generated documents are judged to contain the greatest volume of fabricated content. Chat, despite its freeform nature, produces the least hallucination on average. The standard deviation for hallucination_inverse is lowest in Chat (0.913) and highest in Ask (1.117), suggesting that Chat produces more consistently moderate hallucination behaviour, whereas Ask yields more variable outcomes — some documents closely adhering to the scenario and others substantially departing from it.

## 4. Outlier Examples

The best and worst documents per mode illuminate the ceiling and floor of each interaction style. The worst Form document (id=60) scores completeness=5 alongside hallucination_inverse=1 and overall=1, exemplifying the completeness-faithfulness dissociation: the judge notes the document invents a company name, health data processing, and retention periods of six to ten years against a specified three-month period. The best Form document (id=104) demonstrates that the mode is capable of high-quality output (overall=4, scenario_faithfulness=5), but this appears to be an exception rather than the norm given the low mode mean. The worst Ask document (id=118) similarly scores overall=1 with entirely hallucinated company details, while the best (id=19) achieves overall=4 and legal_correctness=5, though hallucination_inverse=3 indicates residual fabrication of specific technical details such as software tools and RFID systems. Chat's worst document (id=63) scores 1 across all dimensions, with the judge identifying a wholly invented company and placeholder-filled fields; its best (id=11) is the only document in the sample to achieve overall=5, with scenario_faithfulness=5 and legal_correctness=5, demonstrating that freeform conversation can, under favourable conditions, produce the highest-quality outputs observed in the study.

## 5. Within-Mode Variability

Form exhibits the lowest standard deviations across most dimensions — completeness (0.553), legal correctness (0.551), and hallucination_inverse (0.716) — indicating that its structured interface produces consistently patterned outputs, albeit consistently poor on faithfulness and hallucination metrics. Ask and Chat show substantially higher variability. Chat's completeness standard deviation (1.587) and overall standard deviation (1.310) are the highest in their respective columns; Ask's completeness std (1.537) is nearly as high, reflecting that both dialogue-based modes produce highly heterogeneous documents. This variability implies that participant behaviour — how thoroughly they engage with the AI, how accurately they convey scenario details — exerts a stronger influence on document quality in less structured modes. The Chat mode's combination of the highest ceiling (overall=5) and a tied lowest floor (overall=1) encapsulates this dynamic: freeform interaction amplifies both competence and failure, producing the widest effective range of document quality across the sample.
