\section{Confidence and Quality Across Modes}
\label{sec:eval-conf-quality}

This section places the primary self-report confidence measure (item~$5_{ai}$
of the Perceived AI Support instrument) alongside the principal document
quality indicators (BERTScore F1 and Judge Overall) at the mode level, in
preparation for the Discussion chapter's calibration analysis. Per-mode
results are reported in the order Form, Ask, Chat, followed by the aggregated
mean across all documents.

Table~\ref{tab:conf-quality} reports, for the survey × NLP joined sample
($n = 106$ documents, per-mode $n = 34$ Form, $34$ Ask, $38$ Chat), the
mean self-reported legal-basis confidence, the mean document-level BERTScore
F1, and the mean Judge Overall.

For \emph{self-reported confidence}, Form ranks third ($3.38$), Ask second
($3.68$), and Chat first ($3.76$); aggregated $3.61$. For \emph{BERTScore
F1}, Form ranks first ($0.906$), Chat second ($0.904$), and Ask third
($0.896$); aggregated $0.902$. For \emph{Judge Overall}, Form ranks third
($2.38$), Ask second ($2.56$), and Chat first ($2.63$); aggregated $2.52$.
The mode-level rank ordering on legal-basis confidence therefore matches
Judge Overall but is the inverse of BERTScore F1.

Within-mode Spearman correlations between item~$5_{ai}$ and the two quality
measures are reported in Table~\ref{tab:conf-quality-corr}. The pooled
correlations across all $106$ documents are essentially zero. The
mode-specific correlations differ in sign: Form and Ask show non-significant
negative trends, while Chat shows a non-significant positive trend on
BERTScore F1 and a significant positive correlation with Judge Overall
($\rho = +0.41$, $p = 0.012$, $n = 38$).
